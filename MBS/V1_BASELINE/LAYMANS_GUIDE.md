# MKRS BRAIN V1.0 — THE LAYMAN'S GUIDE (Simple Explanation)

Think of the **MKRS Brain** not as code, but as a **Super-Librarian** who has a photographic memory but reads in a very specific way to make sure they never miss a detail.

Here is how the system works, explained with simple analogies.

---

### 1. Reading the Documents (The "Smart Ingest" Phase)
Most AI systems read a PDF like a scanner—they just grab all the words. But if a PDF has two columns, a standard AI reads across both columns, jumbling the sentences. 

**Our System (The Super-Librarian):**
*   **The Column Checker:** Before reading, it looks at the page layout. It says, "Ah, this is a two-column layout like a newspaper." It reads the entire left column first, then the entire right column. 
*   **The Table Specialist:** When it sees a table, it doesn't just read the numbers randomly. It "freezes" the table, keeps it in its original grid, and puts a little note next to it saying what it's about.
*   **The Garbage Filter:** It recognizes repeating headers and footers (like "Page 5" or "Draft Copy") and ignores them so they don't clutter up the answers.

---

### 2. Organizing the Memory (The "Parent-Child" Phase)
Imagine trying to memorize a book. You can't memorize the whole book at once, and memorizing single words is useless because you lose context.

**Our System's Strategy:**
*   **The "Child" Cards (The Index):** The librarian creates tiny cards with 2 sentences on each. These are easy to search through quickly.
*   **The "Parent" Folders (The Context):** Every tiny card is stapled to a large 2-page folder. 
*   **Why?** When you ask a question, the librarian quickly searches the **tiny cards** to find the right topic. Once found, they pull the **entire large folder** out to give the AI the full story. This ensures the answer isn't just a snippet, but a complete, accurate explanation.

---

### 3. Finding the Answer (The "Re-ranking" Phase)
When you ask a question, a standard search might give you 10 documents that "look" like they have the answer because they share the same words. 

**Our System's Secret Sauce:**
*   **Stage 1 (The Speed Runner):** It quickly grabs the top 60 most likely passages.
*   **Stage 2 (The Expert Reviewer):** It then gives those 60 passages to an "Expert Reviewer" who reads them one by one. This expert is slower but much smarter. They pick the **best 3** that actually answer your specific question, not just the ones that have the same words.

---

### 4. Giving the Answer (The "Sarvam-1" Phase)
When it’s time to talk to you, the system gives those 3 best documents to the **Sarvam-1 AI**.

*   **The Instruction:** We tell the AI: "Only talk about what is in these 3 documents. Do not guess. Do not make things up. If the answer isn't there, say you don't know."
*   **The Result:** You get a safe, factual, and extremely accurate answer based *only* on the documents you uploaded.

---

### Summary: Why is this "Industry Grade"?
1.  **It doesn't mix up columns.**
2.  **It understands tables.**
3.  **It checks its own work** (The Expert Reviewer).
4.  **It provides sources** (It tells you exactly which page and document it found the answer on).
