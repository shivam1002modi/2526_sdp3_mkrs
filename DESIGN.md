# Design System: Adobe Acrobat Chat with PDF
**Project ID:** local-acrobat-style

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The design is professional, trustworthy, and airy. It uses a clean white core with a vibrant red brand accent. The overall mood is "Premium Corporate AI" — sophisticated but highly accessible.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Adobe Red (#ec1c24):** Primary brand color, used for the logo and top-of-page atmosphere.
- **Classic White (#ffffff):** Main background and surface color for the central interaction card.
- **Action Blue (#0055ff):** Used for primary call-to-action buttons (e.g., "Select files").
- **Muted Gray (#b1b1b1):** Used for dashed borders, secondary text, and subtle dividers.
- **Deep Slate (#111827):** Primary text color for maximum readability and contrast.
- **Slate Gray (#6b7280):** Secondary text for helper instructions.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Inter/Roboto:** Use a clean, sans-serif font family.
- **Headings:** Bold weights (font-weight: 700) for "Chat with your PDF".
- **Body:** Regular weights (font-weight: 400) for subtext and instructions.
- **Alignment:** Centered layout for the hero content to emphasize focus.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:**
    - **Primary:** Rounded pill shape, Action Blue background, white text, bold.
    - **Secondary/Demo:** Rounded pill shape, transparent background, solid black border, black text.
- **Cards/Containers:**
    - **Central Card:** Large white container with generous rounded corners (24px).
    - **Dashed Dropzone:** A gray dashed border (#b1b1b1) inside the central card to indicate interactivity.
- **Header:** Clean white strip with small Adobe logo and horizontally spaced navigation links.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Atmosphere Header:** A lush red-to-white linear gradient at the top that draws the eye downward.
- **Whitespace:** Ample breathing room around the central card and between text elements.
- **Balance:** Symmetrical alignment for text, with a decorative AI illustration balanced on the right of the action area.
