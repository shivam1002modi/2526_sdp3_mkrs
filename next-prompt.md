A high-fidelity landing page for an AI-powered PDF tool, inspired by Adobe Acrobat's design language. The goal is a professional, premium, and trustworthy interface.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop-first
- Theme: Professional, clean, and airy
- Background: Red-to-White linear gradient (#ec1c24 to #ffffff) starting from the top header area
- Primary Accent: Adobe Red (#ec1c24) for branding and logo
- Secondary Accent: Professional Blue (#0055ff) for primary action buttons
- Surface: Clean White (#ffffff) for the main central card
- Border: Dashed Gray (#b1b1b1) for the file upload dropzone
- Typography: Inter or Roboto, centered for main heading
- Cards: Standard rounded corners (16px), prominent dashed border for the hero section

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header Navigation:** 
   - Logo: A red square with a white curvy 'A' (Acrobat style)
   - Menu: "PDFs & E-signatures", "Tools", "Convert", "Edit", "Sign & Protect", "Generative AI", "Buy now"
   - Right Utility: "Free trial" button (blue pill), "Sign in", and a search icon.
2. **Hero Section:**
   - Background: Vibrant red gradient merging into white below.
   - Main Card: Centered white card with a dashed border.
   - Content:
     - Acrobat Logo icon.
     - Heading: "Chat with your PDF".
     - Subtext: "Get trusted document insights by asking questions with Acrobat AI Assistant."
     - Dropzone instructional text: "Drag and drop one or more PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, or RTF files."
     - CTA Buttons: "Select files" (solid Blue with upload icon) and "Try with a demo file" (outlined black).
   - Illustration: Stylized abstract graphic on the right representing AI interaction (purple/blue chat bubbles).
3. **Footer:** Simple links for Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Cookies.
